The Books Oracle

Three hundred books on your list. Which one will you read tonight?

The Books Oracle is a reading companion for people with more books than evenings. It keeps your wishlist and your library, tracks the series you are partway through, and builds reading plans that pace you through a stack. When you want to be told what to read, the Oracle draws from your own shelf — and always tells you why it chose what it chose. The choice stays yours.

  • Ranked suggestions from books you already own, each with its reasons
  • Reading plans that turn a stack of books into a path
  • Series tracking, book clubs, shared lists and reading friends
  • The Vault — a curated catalog of around a thousand proven titles
  • Fully bilingual: every page exists in English and Spanish
  • Free forever as a Seeker; five Oracle suggestions a month
